AI Sales Coach: Practice Calls and Build Sales Scripts

Practice the conversations that decide your close rate, build a call script for your offer in a few clicks, and work through lessons on objections and closing.

What you can do here

  1. Script Generator: describe your product and buyer, pick their pain points and your benefits, choose a method (SPIN Selling, Challenger, Sandler, Grant Cardone 10X and more) and a tone, and get a full call script with answers to common objections.
  2. Role Play: talk to a prospect who filled out your ad form but forgot what it was about, and practice reopening the conversation.
  3. Training Hub and Dashboard: lessons on closing and objection handling, with levels and points that track your practice.

Questions people ask

Is the AI Sales Coach free?

Yes. You can generate scripts and practice role plays without signing up.

Which sales methods does the script generator use?

SPIN Selling, Challenger Sale, Sandler, Grant Cardone 10X, Baseline Selling and Conceptual Selling.

Who is it for?

Sales reps, appointment setters and business owners who take their own sales calls.

Can I use the scripts on real calls?

Yes. Fill in the bracketed parts with your own numbers and stories, then practice the script out loud a few times before you use it.
